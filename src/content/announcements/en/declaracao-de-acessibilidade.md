Airespucrs.org is committed to providing an accessible website to as broad an audience as possible, regardless of circumstance or ability. Our goal is to adhere as closely as possible to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.0, Level AA), published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities. Compliance with these guidelines will help make the web more user-friendly for everyone. While airespucrs.org strives to adhere to accessibility guidelines and standards, it is not always possible to do so across every area of the site, and we are currently working to achieve this. Please note that, due to the dynamic nature of the site, minor issues may occasionally occur as it is updated regularly. We are always looking for solutions to bring every area of the site up to the same overall level of web accessibility.

If you have any comments or suggestions related to improving the accessibility of our website, please feel free to contact our President, Nicholas Kluge, by phone (+55 51 - 981706812) or email ([nicholas@airespucrs.org](#)). Your feedback will help us make improvements.
