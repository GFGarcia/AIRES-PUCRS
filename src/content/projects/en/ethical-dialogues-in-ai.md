The Ethical Dialogues in AI meeting is a partnership between the AI Robotics Ethics Society (AIRES) and the Network for Safe and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence (RAIES), both based at PUCRS. The project consists of a monthly meeting, held on the last Thursday of each month, from noon to 1pm.

We invite a guest researcher to present their thoughts on a pre-selected text, after which we open the floor for debate with the other participants.

You can follow the meetings live via the [Zoom](https://pucrs.zoom.us/j/92018866882?pwd=VE1Sczk2Wis3bzVwVHJ0VzVtZ0k1Zz09) room.

Recorded sessions are available on our [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AIRESPUCRS) channel.

Monthly announcements are shared on: [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/airespucrs?igsh=MXMxeHlmMGNieTh0bg==) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aires-pucrs/).
