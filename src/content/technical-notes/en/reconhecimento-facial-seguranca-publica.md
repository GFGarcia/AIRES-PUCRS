In 2022, the city government of Porto Alegre announced plans to **expand the use of facial recognition cameras in public spaces**. This type of technology, "_Facial Recognition Technology_" (**FRT**), will be used for public security purposes. But what are the **risks associated with using this type of technology**, and what **should you know before forming an opinion** on the subject?

In this document, titled "_Technical Note: Risks of Using Facial Recognition Technologies for Public Security Purposes_", researchers from the **_AI Robotics Ethics Society_** (AIRES) and the **_Network for Safe and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence_** (RAIES) present arguments spanning **technical** (_regarding the technology itself_), **legal** (_regarding the legality of FRTs_), and **ethical** (_regarding the ethical problems we face when using FRTs_) dimensions.

We hope our work can be used to **inform the discussion about the use of FRTs** for public security purposes.
