This Technical Note critically analyzes **Provisional Measure No. 1,318/2025**, which creates the Special Taxation Regime for Data Center Services in Brazil (REDATA). **The document examines the environmental, energy, territorial, and regulatory impacts of this infrastructure's expansion in the country, highlighting the risks of a model based exclusively on tax incentives, without robust sustainability, transparency, and socio-environmental justice criteria.**

Drawing on Brazilian environmental legislation, the Federal Constitution, international climate commitments, and recent research, **the Note assesses relevant gaps in the government proposal and presents recommendations for more consistent regulation, aligned with sustainable limits and the long-term public interest**.

The goal is to strengthen public debate and provide technical input for shaping a socially just and environmentally responsible national data center policy.
