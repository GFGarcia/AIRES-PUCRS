The Board is currently responsible for a range of projects and activities, including our website, research, social media, events, and meetings, among others. We plan to further expand these projects and integrate new ideas, promoting an ethical culture in the development and use of Artificial Intelligence. Our goal is to involve as many PUCRS student volunteers as possible, from every field, so that everyone can contribute to the mission of AIRES.
